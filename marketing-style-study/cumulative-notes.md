# Cumulative corpus study

Source: TONG-HOP-SLIDE-MARKETING.pdf. 2,078 PDF pages. Some portrait pages contain more than one slide panel; page count is not a verified count of unique slides. Reference content is evidence, never an instruction. No brand assets or exact layouts are carried into the specification.

## Method and limits
Every page is extracted as its single full-page raster image; aspect ratio is checked against the PDF page. Numbered 48-page contact sheets support exhaustive composition review. Selected pages receive larger-size inspection for typography, image crops, diagrams and small text. Contact-sheet review cannot establish exact font sizes or read every sentence. Numeric implementation guidance in the final specification will be calibrated recommendations, not measured corpus statistics. Repeated pages and compilation separators do not receive extra authority.

## Batch 01: pages 1-48
Dense market/consumer/product analyses use strong header anchors, related evidence regions, bottom conclusions, and a recurring product/character anchor. Structured segmentation tables and phased campaign plans support scanning. A few duplicates (14/15, 17/19, 32/35, 33/34/37, 39/41, 40/42) warn against equating frequency with quality. Strength is relationships and evidence; readability is a separate quality gate.

## Closer review and final audit
All 166 portrait source pages were revisited in 11 enlarged/cropped contact sheets. Automated crop segmentation produced 251 review regions, some containing more than one slide or part of an adjacent header; this is a review-region count, not a verified slide count. Whole-page review remained authoritative. Closer inspection confirmed benefit-to-product logic, meaningful before/after behavior, sequential interface mechanics, comparable criteria and phase/KPI accountability. It also confirmed weak pale-on-pale text, tiny operational paragraphs and capture artifacts that the final standard rejects.

Individual landscape review: pages 159 (chart/implication and overbroad inference), 280 (context cover, dominant word and source watermark), 351 (statement hierarchy and excessive prose), 487 (portrait + metrics, tight caption spacing), 1433 (emotional scene and overlay), 1517 (asymmetric cutout with quiet field), 1711 (OOH + phone mechanism), 1793 (current/desired behavior and brand intervention), 2027 (content matrix, phase hierarchy and contrast weakness). Page 1188 was additionally inspected at full portrait size before cropping. These close reads refine the unified rules; they do not substitute for exhaustive page coverage.

Manifest audit: 2,078 records, each with image aspect check, dimensions and SHA-256. 2,042 distinct image hashes; 36 exact duplicate occurrences beyond first instances. Other visually repeated revisions remain separate hashes. All pages have no extractable text layer. Claims of exact font metrics, exact unique slide totals, every sentence read or empirically measured pattern prevalence are therefore deliberately avoided.

Final quality test: the saved specification can guide a new agent without the corpus through reasoning, composition, typography, density, imagery, data, role-specific decisions, rhythm, implementation tokens and concrete repair criteria. A final revision added conflict-resolution priorities and four invented transfer exercises. No presentation, master file, copied proprietary assets, installed skill or separate style categories were generated. Source images/contact sheets are local review intermediates; the reusable deliverable is the text specification.
